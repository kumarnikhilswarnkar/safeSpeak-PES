# AI triage: evaluation and model choice (v3, Phase 2)

Every number here is copied from the reports generated by `ml/scripts/run_experiments.py` in
`ml/reports/v3/` (`model_comparison.md` is the generated summary). Method, layout and commands:
[`ml/README.md`](../ml/README.md). Dataset: [`ml/DATASET.md`](../ml/DATASET.md). The v1/v2 evaluation
is kept as legacy ([`ml/legacy/README.md`](../ml/legacy/README.md)).

## Dataset and protocol

- 600 **synthetic** records (200 AI seeds + 400 AI paraphrases, 200 groups of 3), SHA-256 pinned.
- Holdout: 40 whole groups = 120 records, never used for any decision. Development: 480 records / 160 groups.
- Grouped CV on development: `StratifiedGroupKFold`, 5 folds × 5 repeats (seeds 42–46) = 25 fits per
  candidate per task; no group in both training and validation (asserted and tested).
- Selection (CV only): highest mean macro-F1 → candidates within 1 SD are tied → lowest SD (±0.005) →
  simplest. Calibration (raw / temperature / per-class sigmoid) by cross-fitted log-loss. Threshold per
  task: lowest t with ≥ 80% correct automatic decisions and ≥ 10% automatic (calibrated out-of-fold).
- **Deployment constraint (disclosed, added after the first full run):** the backend must run without
  PyTorch, so only linear-text candidates (2–7) are deployable. The first run's rule selected MiniLM + LR
  for category; deploying it would add about +530 MB to the backend image, +370 MB RAM and +30 s startup
  (measured on the development laptop). The project owner chose the constraint. The same rule is applied
  to the deployable pool, and the unconstrained winner is still reported (`selection.json` → `unconstrained`).
- **SetFit (candidate 9): NOT EVALUATED.** Feasibility gate (`setfit_pilot.json`): one fit must finish in
  600 s. Measured: 212 of 953 training steps after 720.8 s (projected about 3240 s per fit), so it failed.

- The hybrid's evidence shows the frozen keyword-count features carry most of the weight. It is "keyword

- Holdout per-class recall: Exam 0.93, Admin/Fees 0.80, Library/Transport 0.75, Other 0.72,

  0.046). Holdout ECE 0.118.
- Threshold **0.57**: development out-of-fold 57.0% automatic, 80.1% of those correct. Holdout (reported
  only): 72 of 120 automatic, 80.6% correct.

- Selected (same with or without the constraint): **words + chars + LR**. Six models were tied within 1 SD
  of the best mean (word+char SVM, 0.594); it has the lowest SD. It beats the keyword baseline by 6.6 SD.
- MiniLM scored highest on the holdout (70.5% macro-F1) but lowest of the ML models in CV (0.514). The
  holdout is not used for choosing; with 120 records this difference may be chance.
- Holdout: Critical recall **0.81**, High-or-Critical recall 0.65, **19 serious (High/Critical) cases
  predicted as Low/Medium**. High is the weakest class (recall 0.30). This is why every predicted
  High/Critical goes to a human. A High/Critical case predicted as Medium with high confidence can still
  be routed automatically, so human oversight of routed complaints remains necessary.
- Calibration: **per-class sigmoid** (cross-fitted log-loss raw 1.037, temperature 0.910, sigmoid 0.889).
  Holdout ECE 0.139 → 0.097.
- Threshold **0.63**: development out-of-fold 39.8% automatic, 80.7% correct. Holdout (reported only):
  57 of 120 automatic, **71.9% correct, below the 80% target**.

## What the deployed system does

The backend loads exactly `ml/artifacts/v3/deployed.json` (hybrid + words/chars LR, checksums verified).
A complaint goes to human review if category confidence < 0.57, priority confidence < 0.63, or the
predicted priority is High/Critical. On the 5 probe texts: a vague complaint and a safety complaint go to
review, and 3 clear ones are routed automatically (`ml/scripts/predict_demo.py`).

## Honest limitations

- Synthetic data only; real-world accuracy is unknown.
- The category model's gain over hand-written keyword rules is small in CV (0.32 SD).
- Priority prediction is modest (holdout macro-F1 58.7%); the 80% target held on development data but
  not on the holdout (71.9%).
- The deployment constraint was added after seeing results; it is disclosed here and in every report.
